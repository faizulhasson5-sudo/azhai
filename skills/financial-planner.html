The Formula

A = P(1 + r/n)nt + PMT × [((1 + r/n)nt - 1) / (r/n)]

P = initial principal, r = annual rate, n = compounding frequency (12 for monthly), t = years, PMT = monthly contribution. This calculator compounds monthly (n=12).

The 50/30/20 Rule

A simple budgeting framework: spend 50% of after-tax income on needs (housing, food, transport, utilities), 30% on wants (entertainment, dining out, hobbies), and 20% on savings & debt repayment.
