The Prompt Engineering Framework

1. Role

Tell the AI who it should be. "You are a senior copywriter with 15 years of experience in SaaS marketing."

2. Context

Give background information. "I run a small bakery and want to increase online orders."

3. Task

Be specific about what you want. "Write 5 email subject lines for a weekend promotion."

4. Format

Define output structure. "Present as a numbered list with a short rationale for each."

5. Constraints

Set boundaries. "Keep each under 10 words. Avoid clickbait. Target audience is adults 30-50."
