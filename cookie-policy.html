Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences.

Cookies We Use

Necessary Storage

  • theme_preference: Stored in localStorage. Remembers your light/dark mode selection.
  • cookie_consent: Stored in localStorage. Remembers your cookie acceptance or rejection.

Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics)

  • _ga, _ga_* Set by Google Analytics after you accept cookies. Collects anonymous usage statistics.

Note: We use localStorage (not cookies) for theme preference and consent state. Only Google Analytics sets actual cookies, and only after you click "Accept" on the cookie banner.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through:

  • Our cookie banner (shown on first visit)
  • Your browser settings
  • Browser developer tools

Third-Party Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies to collect anonymous usage data. See Google's Privacy Policy.
