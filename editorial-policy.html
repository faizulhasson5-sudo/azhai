Editorial Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Our Commitment to Quality

Every piece of content on KwordSEO is created by experienced professionals, reviewed by peers, and fact-checked against authoritative sources. We believe accurate, trustworthy information is the foundation of good SEO advice.

Our Editorial Process

Every article goes through a rigorous 4-step process before publication:

1
Research

Our writers research topics using primary sources, official documentation, and industry studies. Every claim must be backed by at least one authoritative source.

2
Write

Our writers create comprehensive, original content. We do not publish AI-generated content without human review and expert input. Every article reflects real expertise.

3
Review

Every article is reviewed by at least two team members before publication. Our peer review checks for accuracy, clarity, completeness, and SEO best practices.

4
Publish & Monitor

After publication, we monitor content for accuracy and update articles as needed. Every article shows its last-updated date for transparency.

Fact-Checking Standards

We follow these fact-checking standards:

  • Multiple sources: Every factual claim must be verified against at least one authoritative source (official documentation, peer-reviewed studies, or industry leaders).
  • Primary sources preferred: We cite original research, official Google documentation, and primary data over secondary interpretations.
  • Date awareness: We note when information was last verified and flag content that may be outdated.
  • No speculation as fact: Opinions and predictions are clearly labeled as such. Factual statements are distinguished from analysis.

Source Requirements

Our source requirements for different content types:

  • How-to guides: Must include links to official documentation, tool-specific resources, or verified tutorials.
  • SEO analysis: Must cite Google's official statements, published research papers, or verified case studies.
  • Tool reviews: Must be based on actual hands-on testing, not manufacturer specifications.
  • Industry trends: Must reference original data sources, surveys, or official announcements.

Content Review Process

Before any article is published, it undergoes review by:

  • Subject matter expert: An expert in the topic area verifies technical accuracy.
  • Editorial review: A second team member reviews for clarity, structure, and readability.
  • SEO review: Content is checked for on-page SEO best practices.

Corrections Policy

We are committed to accuracy. If you find an error in our content:

  • Email us at faizulhasson5@gmail.com with the specific error and source.
  • We will review the claim within 48 hours.
  • If confirmed, we will correct the article and update the "last updated" date.
  • Significant corrections will be noted in the article.

AI Content Policy

We use AI tools as drafting assistants, but all content is reviewed, edited, and approved by human experts before publication. AI-generated content is never published without human oversight. Our editorial team ensures every article reflects real expertise and experience.

Update Schedule

We conduct quarterly content audits to identify outdated information. Tool tutorials are updated when tool features change. SEO guides are updated when Google publishes new guidelines. Every article displays its last-updated date prominently.
