SMART Goal Planner

TEACHES: Goal Setting

Turn vague goals into concrete action plans. Walk through the SMART framework step by step.

What is your goal? (Start vague — we'll make it SMART)
S Step 1 of 5 — Specific

What exactly do you want to achieve?

A specific goal is clear and unambiguous. Instead of "get healthier," think "lose 15 pounds" or "run a 5K without stopping." The more precise, the better.

Examples of Specific Goals

  • "Lose 15 pounds in 12 weeks through diet and exercise"
  • "Complete an online Python course and build 3 projects"
  • "Save $10,000 for an emergency fund"
M Step 2 of 5 — Measurable

How will you track progress?

Measurable goals have concrete numbers or milestones. This lets you know when you've succeeded and tracks your progress along the way.

Metrics Suggestions

  • Weekly weigh-ins (every Monday morning)
  • Number of workouts completed per week
  • Calories logged daily
  • Miles run per week increasing by 0.5 mi/month
A Step 3 of 5 — Achievable

Is this realistic for you?

An achievable goal stretches you but is still possible. Consider your current resources, constraints, and what similar people have accomplished.

Reality Check Benchmarks

  • Safe weight loss: 1-2 lbs per week (12-24 lbs in 12 weeks)
  • Learning to code: 3-6 months to build basic apps with daily practice
  • Building savings: Automating 10-20% of income is a proven strategy
R Step 4 of 5 — Relevant

Why does this matter to you?

A relevant goal aligns with your values, long-term vision, and current priorities. It should matter enough to sustain your motivation when things get tough.

Value Alignment Questions

  • Does this support my bigger life goals?
  • Is now the right time for this commitment?
  • Will achieving this make my life or others' lives better?
T Step 5 of 5 — Time-bound

By when will you achieve this?

A deadline creates urgency and helps you plan backwards. Without one, goals tend to drift indefinitely.

Deadline Suggestions

  • Short-term: 4-12 weeks (build momentum)
  • Medium-term: 3-6 months (realistic skill building)
  • Long-term: 6-12 months (significant life change)

About This Tool

This tool teaches you the SMART goal-setting framework used by coaches, managers, and high performers worldwide. It transforms vague wishes into structured, actionable goals.

The SMART Framework

Specific — Clear and unambiguous
Measurable — Has numbers or milestones
Achievable — Realistic for you
Relevant — Aligns with your values
Time-bound — Has a deadline
