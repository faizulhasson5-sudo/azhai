How to Calculate SEO ROI

Understanding the formulas behind SEO return on investment helps you make better marketing budget decisions.

1. Monthly SEO Revenue Formula

Revenue = Organic Traffic × Conversion Rate × Average Order Value

This formula calculates how much revenue your organic search traffic generates each month. For example, 10,000 monthly visitors × 2.5% conversion rate × $50 average order value = $12,500 per month.

2. Monthly ROI Formula

ROI % = ((Revenue - Cost) / Cost) × 100

This measures the profitability of your SEO spend. An ROI of 200% means you earn $2 for every $1 spent on SEO. A negative ROI indicates your SEO investment costs more than it generates.

3. 12-Month Projected Traffic

Traffic(month n) = Initial Traffic × (1 + Growth Rate)^n

Organic traffic compounds monthly. If you start at 10,000 visitors and grow 10% monthly, after 12 months you will have approximately 31,384 monthly visitors — more than triple your starting traffic.

4. Break-Even Point

Break-even = Monthly Cost / (Monthly Revenue per Visitor)

The break-even point tells you how many months it takes before your cumulative SEO revenue exceeds your cumulative SEO cost. With consistent growth, SEO typically breaks even in 2-4 months.

Why SEO ROI Compounds

Unlike paid advertising, where traffic stops the moment you stop paying, SEO builds lasting value. Each month of investment adds to your organic traffic base. This compounding effect means your ROI improves significantly over time — the longer you invest, the higher your returns become.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SEO ROI? +
SEO ROI (Return on Investment) measures the profitability of your SEO efforts. It compares the revenue generated from organic search traffic against the cost of your SEO activities. A positive ROI means your SEO investment is generating more money than it costs.
How do you calculate SEO ROI? +
The formula for SEO ROI is: ((SEO Revenue - SEO Cost) / SEO Cost) × 100. SEO Revenue is calculated by multiplying your monthly organic traffic by your conversion rate and average order value. For example, if you spend $2,000/month on SEO and generate $6,000 in revenue, your ROI is ((6,000 - 2,000) / 2,000) × 100 = 200%.
What is a good SEO ROI? +
A good SEO ROI varies by industry, but most businesses see an ROI of 200-600% from SEO. The average is around 500% according to industry studies. Compared to paid advertising (typically 200-400% ROI), SEO tends to deliver higher long-term returns because organic traffic compounds over time.
How long does it take to see ROI from SEO? +
Most businesses begin seeing measurable SEO ROI within 3-6 months of consistent effort. However, significant returns typically take 6-12 months. SEO is a long-term strategy — unlike paid ads, the traffic compounds over time, meaning your ROI improves each month without additional proportional spending.
What factors affect SEO ROI the most? +
The key factors affecting SEO ROI are: conversion rate (higher = more revenue per visitor), average order value (higher = more revenue per conversion), monthly organic traffic volume, cost of SEO services or in-house team, and the monthly growth rate of organic traffic. Improving any of these variables directly impacts your ROI.
