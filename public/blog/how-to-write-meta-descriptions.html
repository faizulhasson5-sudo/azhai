Meta Description Templates: 15 Copy-Paste Examples for Every Page Type

2026-07-06 SEO Tutorial

Why Templates Save Time

Writing meta descriptions from scratch for every page is inefficient. Templates give you proven structures that you customize for your specific page. The result: consistent, high-CTR meta descriptions across your entire site.

The 5-Step Template Process

Step 1: Identify the page type (blog, product, category, landing page, etc.). Step 2: Choose the matching template below. Step 3: Fill in the bracketed placeholders. Step 4: Add your primary keyword naturally. Step 5: Verify it is under 155 characters.

Blog Post Templates

How-to Guide: Learn how to [action] with this step-by-step guide. Includes [number] practical tips and real examples. Updated for [year].

Listicle: Discover [number] [things] that [benefit]. Each one is [tested/proven] to [result]. See which ones work best.

Comparison: Compare [A] vs [B] - features, pricing, pros, and cons. Find the best [category] for your needs.

Product Page Templates

E-commerce: [Product] - [key benefit]. [Price point] with [differentiator]. [Social proof]. Free shipping on orders over $[amount].

SaaS: [Product] helps [audience] [achieve result]. [Key feature]. Start with free trial. No credit card required.

Landing Page Templates

Lead magnet: Download the free [resource] to [achieve result]. [Number] pages of [value description]. Get instant access.

Webinar: Join [number]+ [audience] for [topic]. Learn [number] strategies from [expert]. Register free - limited spots.

Service and Category Templates

Service page: [Service] for [audience]. [Number] years experience. Get a free consultation.

Category page: Browse [number]+ [items] in [category]. Filter by [criteria]. Shop now.

Testing Your Templates

Use our free Meta Tag Generator to preview how descriptions appear in search results. Monitor CTR in Google Search Console for 2-4 weeks after updating.

Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Using the same template for every page. Different page types need different approaches.

Mistake 2: Forgetting the keyword. Every template should naturally include your primary keyword.

Mistake 3: Being generic. Templates are starting points. Customize them with specific details.
