What is an Ads Quality Checker?

A comprehensive tool to evaluate and improve your advertising copy before spending money on ads.

Analyze Every Ad Before You Launch

An ads quality checker evaluates your ad copy across proven marketing frameworks — AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action) and PAS (Problem, Agitate, Solve) — along with readability, CTA strength, emotional appeal, and platform-specific best practices. Instead of guessing which ad will perform best, you get data-driven insights that help you choose the highest-converting variation before spending a single dollar on ad spend.

Why Compare Multiple Ad Variations?

Top advertisers never run a single ad variant. They create 3-4 variations of headlines, body text, and CTAs, then test them against each other. This tool gives you a head start by scoring each variation across six dimensions, showing you exactly where each ad is strong or weak, and recommending which one to run first. A/B testing with data beats guessing every time.

100% Private — Your Data Stays in Your Browser

All analysis runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your ad copy is never sent to any server, API, or third party. This means you can safely analyze sensitive campaign copy, client ads, or competitive research without any privacy concerns.

How to Use This Tool

Step 1: Select Your Platform

Choose the ad platform you're creating for — Facebook, Instagram, Google Search, Google Display, TikTok, LinkedIn, or Twitter/X. Each platform has specific character limits and best practices that the tool uses for scoring.

Step 2: Enter Your Ad Copy

Add 1 to 4 ad variations. Each ad has three fields: Headline (the main hook), Body (primary text or description), and Call-to-Action (the CTA button text or phrase). You can add or remove ads as needed.

Step 3: Analyze and Compare

Click "Analyze All Ads" to get instant scores. The tool evaluates each ad across six dimensions, gives a composite score and grade, and recommends the best performer. Review the dimension breakdown and specific recommendations to improve weaker ads.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ads quality checker? +
An ads quality checker is a tool that evaluates ad copy across multiple dimensions like AIDA framework compliance, PAS framework, readability, CTA strength, and emotional appeal. It scores each ad and helps you identify which variation will perform best before spending money on ads.
How does the AIDA scoring work? +
The AIDA score evaluates four components: Attention (headline power words, numbers, questions), Interest (second-person language, empathy words), Desire (benefit language, specificity with numbers), and Action (strong CTAs with action verbs and urgency). Each component is scored and weighted to produce an overall AIDA score.
Can I compare multiple ad variations? +
Yes, the tool supports 1-4 ad variations. Each ad is scored independently across all dimensions, then compared side by side. The tool declares a winner and explains why it performs better than the other variations.
Which ad platforms are supported? +
The tool supports Facebook, Instagram, Google Search, Google Display, TikTok, LinkedIn, and Twitter/X. Each platform has specific length guidelines that are factored into the scoring.
Is my ad data sent to any server? +
No. All analysis happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your ad copy never leaves your device. There are no external API calls or data transmission of any kind.
